## Match the crew to the actual inventory

A two-person crew is the usual starting point for a studio or one-bedroom apartment. A two-bedroom move generally starts with three movers. Three-bedroom and larger household moves start with four. Furniture volume, packing needs, and access can change the plan, so send the item list rather than choosing only from the bedroom label.

A lightly furnished one-bedroom and a one-bedroom with a large book collection are different jobs. Boxes matter. So do storage cages, outdoor furniture, and the desk you moved into the spare corner last year.

The lowest hourly rate does not by itself tell you which crew will produce the lowest total. Ask the team to explain its recommendation against your inventory and access details.

## Separate labor from the other charges

Splendid's local moving work includes a truck, furniture blankets, disassembly and reassembly, and packing and unpacking labor. Tape and shrink wrap are included. Boxes, bubble wrap, and other additional materials can carry a separate charge. Confirm the packing plan and materials before comparing totals.

The current gas-fee schedule uses the driving circuit from the Los Angeles base to pickup, then to delivery, and back to the base. It is not simply the distance between your two homes.

Long-distance moves need an individual pricing conversation based on the route and shipment. Confirm which rates, documents, and terms apply before comparing a long-distance price with a local hourly calculation.

## Send the details that affect the work

Include both full addresses, floor numbers, elevator availability, and the approximate walk from the truck to each door. Photograph bulky furniture and mention anything that needs disassembly. Say which rooms you want help packing.

For an apartment with stairs, the [walk-up moving guide](/blog/moving-without-elevator-los-angeles) explains what to measure. Better access details make the planning conversation more useful than a guessed total based on square footage alone.
