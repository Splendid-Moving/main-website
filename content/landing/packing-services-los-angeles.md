## List what needs packing in each room

"One kitchen" could mean a few shelves of everyday dishes or cupboards of glassware and small appliances. Take photos with cabinet doors open. Show the pantry and the items on top of the cabinets if those are moving too.

For bedrooms and living spaces, include books, framed items and anything in drawers that needs attention. Point out fragile pieces and awkward shapes. Photos help explain the request; they do not replace a discussion of how a particular item should be handled.

Make a separate note for items you will carry yourself, such as medication, identity documents, keys and your laptop. Keep them away from the packing area when the crew arrives.

## Confirm which materials your move needs

We bring tape and shrink wrap, furniture blankets and wardrobe boxes for use during the move. Boxes and bubble wrap may add to the charge. Confirm the materials and quantities with the team rather than assuming every room needs the same supplies.

Packing labor takes time. For local residential work, the published two-mover rate is $115 per hour with cash or $125 with a card, with a three-hour minimum. That is the local moving service's hourly rate. The exact packing work needs its own scope in your moving quote. Ask for the packing scope to be reflected in your actual quote.

## Pack the kitchen in stages

Set aside what you need for the last evening and first morning. Keep a kettle or coffee setup, a cup, basic utensils and cleaning supplies accessible. You can then finish packing the rest without reopening several boxes.

Use box sizes you can carry, especially for dishes and books. A box can be full before it is manageable. Label fragile contents and destination rooms on the sides, where the labels remain visible in a stack. ## Keep unpacking decisions in the plan

If you want unpacking help, explain what that means for your move. Placing boxes in the correct rooms, opening them and putting contents away are different tasks. Agree on the work instead of leaving it to be decided at the new address.
