# Splendid Moving copy context

This is the factual brief for website and SEO copy. Use the existing quote rules and current business records for claims; do not turn illustrative examples into customer stories.

The customer is an LA renter, homeowner or office manager planning a move. The primary action is to request a free quote at `/quote`, with the phone number `(323) 645-2636` as the alternative. Organic visitors may arrive with a narrow question about an apartment, stairs, packing, building paperwork, office inventory or price.

## Offer and verified details

- Local residential crews: two movers $115 cash/$125 card hourly; three $145/$155; four $180/$190. All have a three-hour minimum.
- Truck, furniture blankets, wardrobe boxes for use during the move, disassembly/reassembly, and packing/unpacking labor are part of the documented offer. Boxes and bubble wrap can cost extra. Follow the quote's materials wording when uncertain.
- Gas fees depend on the full driving circuit. Ask about applicable driving time and other quote details; do not promise a labor subtotal is the whole bill.
- Offices and long-distance moves are quoted from their actual details. The application's 250-mile round-trip threshold is an internal quoting rule, not a legal definition of an interstate move.
- Address: 550 N Figueroa St, Los Angeles CA 90012. Email: info@splendidmoving.com. Hours: daily 6AM–11PM.
- COIs can be requested. A building decides its requirements and accepts the paperwork; do not promise universal acceptance or turnaround times.
- TVs can be dismounted, wrapped and transported; installation at the new property is not offered.

## Voice and proof boundaries

Professional, conversational and concrete. Write as someone helping a customer prepare an item list and work out access. Use the `copywriting` skill. Avoid generic reassurance, staged contrast lines, keyword repetition, tidy motivational endings and made-up anecdotes.

Do not invent licensing identifiers, guaranteed arrival/finish times, insurance terms, discounts, storage services, crane work, specialist IT services or completed jobs. Keep genuine existing testimonials visible, but don't combine third-party ratings into self-serving review schema. A free quote is the next step; claims about Google rankings are never customer-facing copy.

## Learnings

The November 2026 audit found missing old-URL redirects and a large hero image; it did not justify replacing the entire site's voice. Existing service pages already cover many practical questions. New pages need distinct customer intent and useful original detail rather than neighborhood-name substitutions.
